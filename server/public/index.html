Safety boundaries

Controls enforced by the server, not by this interface.

  • AI interpretation is advisory only. It never authorizes, approves, initiates, or captures a payment.
  • Deterministic policy is authoritative: currency, ceiling, allowlist, expiry, and approval rules are re-evaluated by the server.
  • Operator approval is explicit and recorded before capture. A browser click alone is never treated as approval.
  • Capture is verified independently against provider evidence before it is recorded as successful.
  • UNKNOWN states fail closed. They are never silently retried or converted into success.
  • Audit events are append-only and retrieved through an authenticated endpoint.

  • Approve or capture automatically.
  • Treat AI output as authorization.
  • Retry an UNKNOWN capture state.
  • Store the operator token or log payment secrets.
  • Create history records that the server did not return.
